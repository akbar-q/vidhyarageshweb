Poetry section

Names Like Constellations

Some names arrive like light.
They make corridors feel kinder,
make difficult work feel possible,
and leave behind a trail of brave.
This page is made of those names.

Mentors in Motion

They teach without shrinking you,
correct without crushing you,
and celebrate progress out loud.
Their belief becomes your backbone, one day at a time.

Future Makers

Not every hero wears a cape.
Some carry attendance sheets,
notebooks, late-evening patience,
and the stubborn hope that you can do it.
They are right, every time.

For every woman here: thank you for making brilliance feel possible.
