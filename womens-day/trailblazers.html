Marie Curie ⚛️

Pioneered radioactivity research and became the first person to win two Nobel Prizes.

Ada Lovelace 💻

Imagined algorithmic computing long before modern computers existed.

Katherine Johnson 🛰️

Her calculations helped launch early US space missions safely into orbit.

Kalpana Chawla 🌌

An astronaut and inspiration who showed generations that dreams can cross galaxies.

Frida Kahlo 🎨

Turned pain into art and transformed visual storytelling forever.

Malala Yousafzai 📚

Advocates for girls’ education globally with fearless voice and action.
